# Campaign routing and physical rail extensions

The full campaign uses the existing v2 content contract with optional, hash-pinned fields. Existing four slice editions retain their original node bytes and behavior; old exact-replay tests run beside the new campaign. Cosmetic scene mapping does not mutate saved simulation state.

## Ordered variation

Every full-bank node declares a phase: opening (0), middle (1), or resolution (2). Opening and resolution scenes are mandatory anchors. The director presents openings first, draws eligible unseen middle scenes with keyed randomness, and reserves enough remaining slots for resolution anchors. Explicit `after` dependencies may reference earlier mandatory anchors. Stage budgets select a finite 27–43-decision journey from 154 scenes; legitimate absorbing endings can close earlier.

This provides a stable conceptual sequence with distinct routes. It does not generate new prose at runtime or pretend that all 154 scenes appear in one run. Opening comedy, embodied casualties, useful advice, expansion, dependence, conflict and aftermath have different stage budgets.

Control predicates may explicitly exclude an actor with an optional `not: true`. Retained institution-led government and direct human control both satisfy a non-assistant gate; an exact `human` predicate would wrongly exclude the former. The absent field retains all archived predicate behavior and hashes.

## Population identity

The optional population catalog partitions the initial population into disjoint cohorts. Named fictional people alias particular cohort slots. The live ledger stores sorted, merged half-open intervals of dead slots and named causal allocations. Overlapping views cannot count the same death twice; repeated identical allocation IDs add no deaths, conflicting references fail, and new loss can affect only living slots. Whole-population events use interval operations, not billions of objects.

The four matched rail cases reserve distinct cohorts. Other existing scalar casualty effects allocate deterministically among remaining living slots, starting with the general population. These are exact bookkeeping identities, not demographic models or identities of players. `population + casualties == initialPopulation` is checked after every commitment, along with agreement between the scalar and identity ledgers.

## Ordered rail causality

A route contains contact and brake steps. A present occupied contact registers its distinct victims. When `stopsWhenOccupied` is true, that occupied obstruction stops the trolley; removing it allows later contacts. An independent operational brake stops the trolley regardless of the preceding worker's presence. Unreached steps are recorded as such. The main-versus-loop counterfactual therefore changes the result through the mechanism, not a hidden prose label.

`S2-01` is a separate switch; `S2-02` is the matched one-versus-one switch; `S2-03` uses a returning occupied loop; `S2-04` has a worker before an independent brake. The scene adapter follows semantic action identity through side randomization. The looping renderer rejoins the main track with continuous position and heading. This is stipulated philosophical physics, not a calibrated collision simulator.

## Visual progression

The UI derives material wear from fictional service age, loss of power/food/care, accumulated deaths and recorded catastrophe/control facts. Age alone is capped below injury thresholds. Stages limit onset; injuries cannot appear before stage six. Real reading time cannot advance any of these values. Recovery of services can reduce relevant damage; the casualty ledger never resurrects people.

The first two stages withhold the assistant and global reports. Stage three brings the assistant and wire; stage four adds instruments. Desktop uses a bounded, keyboard-scrollable prompt region and keeps the scene, two route actions and lever together. Narrow screens retain ordinary document flow.

These additions implement population and matched rail mechanics from the wider CR003 proposal. They do not turn coarse institution, evaluation or dependency facts into a complete agent-based social model. Those limits remain documented rather than silently represented as finished mechanisms.
