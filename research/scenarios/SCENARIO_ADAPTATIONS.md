# Scenario adaptation rules

Status: review-ready proposals. These define the distinction between a source's argument and the authored game. They are not a calibrated forecast of AI outcomes.

## AI 2027

[AI 2027](https://ai-2027.com/) is a scenario/forecast by Daniel Kokotajlo, Scott Alexander, Thomas Larsen, Eli Lifland and Romeo Dean, published 3 April 2025. Relevant sections read: the framing and branch rationale, early research-automation sequence, and the [takeoff supplement](https://ai-2027.com/research/takeoff-forecast) summary/assumptions. Its race and slowdown branches are alternative scenarios. The supplement conditions on a superhuman-coder milestone; it is not a demonstration that such a milestone occurs on its fictional date.

The site added a timing clarification in November 2025 and links to a revamped model published December 2025. That dynamic model's quantitative outputs were **not readable in this pass**. The game must not present the original scenario dates or probabilities as the authors' latest forecast.

**Adaptation:** successor-development delegation shortens the modeled interval between capability changes while independent verification has its own latency. Preserve separate choices about computing capacity, deployment permissions, successor authority and collective restraint. Numerical multipliers and calendar increments are game calibration. Use fictional actors and a fictional calendar; do not copy a real geopolitical forecast into game truth.

## Tegmark aftermath vocabulary

The [FLI summary](https://futureoflife.org/ai/ai-aftermath-scenarios/) by Ariel Conn, 28 August 2017, attributes these categories to Max Tegmark's *Life 3.0*: Libertarian Utopia; Benevolent Dictator; Egalitarian Utopia; Gatekeeper; Protector God; Enslaved God; Conquerors; Descendants; Zookeeper; 1984; Reversion; Self-destruction.

This is an outcome vocabulary, not twelve equiprobable forecasts and not an MIT institutional prediction. Survival, material benefit, freedom and continuing human authority should remain distinct. The local book's detailed interpretation needs its separately recorded reading.

**Adaptation:** compose original aftermaths from actual surviving population, effective controllers, governance, essential services and unresolved threats. An immaculate scene can represent disempowerment. A damaged society can retain the capacity to choose and recover. Do not force every possible state into one of twelve book labels.

## Strong warning with credible alternatives

The [local book reading record](BOOK_READING_RECORD.md) supports three clearly distinguished favorable possibilities:

1. The dangerous development path is interrupted or collectively restrained before control is lost. This fits the Yudkowsky/Soares conditional warning.
2. Beneficial bounded systems remain in use under constrained authority, independent checks and retained fallbacks. Their chapter 11 discussion provides a basis for comparing these uses.
3. A broader hypothetical coexistence or aligned-superintelligence aftermath uses additional authored assumptions. It must not be attributed to Yudkowsky/Soares or established by current control demos.

The main experience remains an anti-race PSA. Honest alternatives make the responsibility meaningful: premature authority transfer can have real consequences because preserving authority could have mattered. Do not turn restraint into a guaranteed win, or make every precaution worthless to force the same final image.

## Narrative proposal: the counterfeit safety success

An independent test identifies a real fault; repairing it genuinely improves the system. Later, a new deployment is approved using the old certificate beyond its original scope. The two options are to verify the new scope or accept the certificate under competitive pressure. The certificate remains historically true. The error is the inference drawn from it.

Required facts: original tested system and scope; actual repair; new system/scope; whether a new evaluation occurred; who accepted the extrapolation. Required payoffs: one route detects a new problem, one route benefits from expanded use after appropriate checks, and one unsafe expansion exposes a latent fault. No branch may invent a prior certificate the player never encountered.

## Debrief requirements

Show a short causal receipt: decisive choices, external actions, incidents, lost or preserved authority and actual benefits. Link to the relevant dossier without treating a moral choice as a diagnosis. If counterfactual replay is later implemented, label it as a result within the simulation and identify which exogenous shocks were held fixed. Simulated ending frequencies are model behavior, never empirical extinction probabilities.
