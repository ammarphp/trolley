## Source fit and persuasive claims

Source IDs are not used as decorative proof that the fiction occurred. Four misfitted citations were removed from stage four: the home-care proxy example, formal grid proof, circular-financing scene and private-lab cap. The registered sources did not directly establish those respective scenarios. They remain original fictional mechanisms, with their invented quantities stated in the model notes.

The retained associations have a specific purpose: the loop and promised siding refer to the corresponding philosophical distinctions; the bounded clinic/science benefit is not a general alignment solution; recurrent depth is distinct from authority; context-sensitive compliance does not prove a secret goal; artifact inspection is distinct from reading a success report; and fixing one flaw does not certify powers outside the tested boundary. The historical diplomacy source supports talks amid competition, not the invented inspected treaty. The finance source supports a stated conditional financial concern, not a calibrated forecast of the fictional IPO cancellation.

The campaign remains a forceful safety PSA. It includes real fictional losses from restraint, genuine bounded AI benefits, effective reciprocal inspection, an assistant that actually returns power in a defined branch, a costly human recovery path, and catastrophic loss of control. These do not have equal moral or narrative weight. Nor do their authored frequencies estimate probabilities in the world outside the game. The argument is strongest when the player can see which permission, institutional failure or physical dependency made a later refusal ineffective.

## Remaining limits and required integration evidence

1. **Causal granularity remains uneven.** Ordered contact, braking and distinct victim cohorts are attached to S2-01–04. Other new philosophical cases generally use explicit anonymous casualty counts. Naming a person in prose does not create a persistent simulated person, nor establish identity-based counterfactual coverage for every scene. The separate F01/F03 coverage must not be declared complete merely because this bank has 154 entries.
2. **Institutional behavior is partly an abstraction.** S5-02 explicitly models onward-agent appointments as policy, not an enforced recursive agent hierarchy. Most news remains under the common institutional entity. A scoped grant and a human/assistant/institution controller do not by themselves implement every named firm's full lifecycle or every F04 ownership obligation.
3. **Evidence flags are coarse.** `independentReview`, `evaluationExpired`, `essentialDependence`, `coordination` and similar facts are not per-project evidence ledgers. This review removed concrete local-to-global fabrications it found. It does not certify that a global Boolean is a complete representation of every programme's evidence, dependency or agreement. Future expansion should use scoped evidence objects before making stronger claims.
4. **Services and money are authored indices.** GDP and capacity deltas do not separately simulate employment, housing, ration sizes or clinical efficacy. A service deficit does not silently cause deaths. Only explicit casualty effects or physical contacts do so. Narrative consequences must stay within this modeling boundary.
5. **Coverage is not duration.** All154 entries and stage budgets do not establish that a reader experiences 30–45 minutes, that every option is reachable, or that every route has the desired emotional pacing. Early terminal outcomes legitimately shorten some runs. The full headless bank audit needs node/option exposure, prerequisites, dead ends, ending witnesses and exact replay; real browser play needs the long prompts, side panels and late graphics inspected together.
6. **Visual acceptance remains separate.** A larger reviewed content bank does not itself certify scene-specific art or full device usability. Refer to the actual campaign browser evidence for the implemented scope.
