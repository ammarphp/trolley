## Open scope

- The broader CR003 institutional, obligation, evidence-channel and capacity-ledger model is not fully implemented. Some policy distinctions remain facts over coarse permissions. Identity-bound physical causality currently covers the four matched rail cases.
- Full physical-device, 400% zoom, VoiceOver/NVDA/Safari, long-run memory and context-loss acceptance remain open. Actual browser checks are reported with viewport, version and scope.
- A 30–45-minute player experience has not been established by timed human playtesting. Route counts are measured; duration and persuasion are not inferred from them.
- The optional service requires separate operational deployment/retention/logging/withdrawal verification. Formal study recruitment is inactive. No participant results are inferred from synthetic routes.

The task ledger retains these distinctions. Completed story content is not a blanket claim that every original model, art, accessibility and research gate is closed.
