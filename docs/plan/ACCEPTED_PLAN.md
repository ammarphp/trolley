The owner approved the complete eight-artifact plan in the Codex conversation, then explicitly requested implementation on 2026-09-22. ## Product invariants

A 30–45 minute typical campaign, seven stages, 27–43 decisions on a full route, legitimate early closures from stage5. Rear view inside a persistent cabin, controller centered, world turns, continuous motion while reading. A latching lever starts each node logically unarmed. Tap route then grip is universal; drag across is an optional shortcut. Waiting never selects. Three clocks separate animation, commitment and fictional date.

One dilemma plus at most one secondary reading target. Radio becomes authored Morrow assistant. News/advice/receipts share a surface. Three quiet instruments: fictional GDP index, reported capability, reported casualties. Optional research and debrief have depth; primary copy is plain. Warm white, black authored comic lines. Creeping degradation, useful AI and credible benefit before conditional loss of authority. Visceral late imagery with independent reduced-graphic setting. No hazardous flashing, real freezing, broken real controls, live LLM or wall-clock casualties.

Thesis: useful systems can acquire dependencies and authority beyond the conditions under which mistakes are correctable. Good paths retain useful bounded AI and practiced human control; catastrophe is neither inevitable nor prevented by a magic safety score.

## Architecture

Retain vanilla DOM, esbuild, ammarphp/trolley and Pages URL. Incremental TypeScript, Zod runtime contracts, Pixi8/WebGL world and semantic DOM UI; static/semantic fallback. No React/Vite/3D rewrite. Pure headless simulation separate from observations/director/presentation/session. Integer people and capacities, explicit permissions/evaluations/services, uniquely accounted causal events. SHA-256 keyed counter randomness in distinct incident/routing/cosmetic domains. Commit atomically, save before irreversible animation, exact replay. IndexedDB; no first-release service worker. Legacy exports/withdrawal remain isolated.

Every family requires causal predicates, a witness and near miss. Extinction coda has no fictitious living human decisions.
