ORIGINAL OWNER BRIEF (historical instructions; latest direction above controls):
Okay. We’re going to take a step back and recalibrate the
spec from ground up.

The principle is straightforward. It’s a trolley-problem
simulator that begins extremely innocuous, slowly becomes more sinister, and
reveals itself as a dark, insidious, oppressive AI safety parable. The tonal
trajectory is intended to be: lighthearted curiosity to off-putting academic to
twisted comedy to dark and disturbing to dawning horror dystopian.

The idea is to base this on:

1.    
Real trolley problem scenarios and variations
(that involve the controller). The goal is to show some genuine thought and
engage with some of the actual philosophical dilemmas ever since Judis Jarvis
Thomson gifted the world with the trolley problem and how they’ve evolved. Take
some of the inspiration from MIT’s Moral Machine project, which should inspire
this meaningfully, but obviously this is intended to be a little bit more
abstract and a little bit less about self-driving cars.

2.    
Real literature, especially recent literature,
on AI Doomsday scenarios. For this, I would look at: “If Anyone Builds it,
Everyone Dies” (copy on this local machine), the MIT 12 AI Aftermath Scenarios,
the AI 2027 paper (both of the latter taken online). The idea is for the
trolley problem to be woven into this. It should look at real published
research on AI Safety dilemmas, tradeoffs, and problems that real researchers
would face. This should be interwoven with the trolley problem such that it
becomes something like decision gates that procedurally lead to certain
outcomes. I would probably take some inspiration from the 12 AI Aftermath
Scenarios, AI 2027, or other scenario predictions as the end-states, with certain
flexible parameters that are run-customized. Will dive deeper into this later.

The idea is for it to be legitimately publishable-grade as
both a) a video game, b) a high-tech webpage, c) a thesis/capstone project, d)
a research paper. I will talk about each of these dimensions in some more
detail.

First, on the video game aspect, as this is easier to
discuss.

The goal is for the experience to be legitimately immersive.
This means graphical fidelity, smooth animations, varied icons, bespoke
animations, and extremely dedicated and consistent design philosophy.

Fundamentally, the gameplay loop is simple. Scenario posed
in front of the trolley controller. The player picks one of two options. This is
so simple that there are many intensely creative ways to mess around with it
and vary it. We will get to those. But, fundamentally, we have to make this
part of the experience as immersive and high-fidelity as possible, just because
getting this perfect means that the rest of the project lands. If this isn’t
good enough, then the rest of the project is pointless.

With that established, I’ll share my vision for the primary
interface.

Start screen. Minimal information, just a brief

Rear view of the trolley controller. From

Standard animations of moving left or right.

Constant motion. The tracks are always
