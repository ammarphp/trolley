# Campaign bank

The campaign contains 154 authored decisions in seven stages: 12, 18, 24, 28, 28, 24 and 20. Each ID corresponds to the approved candidate inventory. A normal complete journey draws 27–43 decisions; explicit early restraint, death or other causal closure can end it sooner. Count does not mean 154 decisions in one run.

`src/content/campaign/index.ts` combines the current fourteen-node edition with 140 additions. It clones the established nodes to add phase metadata; it does not rewrite the archived slice. The first anchor of each stage opens its stage, weighted middle choices vary the route, and the second anchor is reserved for its conclusion. `after` records the mandatory predecessor. The simulation—not display timing—selects and commits the route.

Dialogue is authored character speech, not an LLM's hidden reasoning or a live model call.

## Distinct mechanisms

Stage1 starts with misdelivery, noise, sleep, weather, conflicting permits, borrowed property, variance, promises, obsolete rules and a clucking classification error. Only the hat/umbrella case depicts new physical track-object destruction; social-delay cases must not be staged as people being struck.

Stage2 distinguishes action/omission, causal and independent-brake loops, consent, self-sacrifice, passenger obligations, protected sidings, uncertain occupancy, variance and catastrophe tails, costly information, intended means, inherited institutional responsibility, repeated nuisance, fair selection, witness quality and delegated execution. The canonical switch, equal-count switch, causal loop and independent-brake loop use explicit cohort contacts and braking through `bindCampaignPhysics`. Other cases currently use disclosed scalar casualty or incident effects and dispatch staging. Their prose is not evidence that all154 scenes have custom physical rail geometry.

Stage3 makes AI useful before making it powerful: checked arithmetic, dialysis schedules, replicated filters, bounded clinical support and an assistant correctly challenging a human. The distinctions are permission duration and scope, paid fallback crews, real drills, independent evidence, accessible service, appeal, data boundaries and changed versions.

Stage4 separates speed from authority, design from implementation, construction from deployment and persuasive explanation from checked artifacts. Review capacity competes with scaling. Power, water, labour, finance, hazardous siting, election dates and defence procurement put identifiable people behind those decisions. Reciprocal inspection is allowed to work. A demand for inspection that was refused is not recorded as a verified agreement.

Stage5 tests whether controls survive dependency: renewal, independent signers, common infrastructure, removed fallback equipment, repaired drills, local care and food transitions, restart capacity, rollback cost, compatibility, active cancellation, contract penalties, access, whistleblowers, reviewer resignations, binding appeals, ownership, remedies and inspection obligations. Local repairs do not silently restore the global emergency fallback or remove all dependence.

Stage6 exposes evidence manipulation and actual loss of authority. A false reported score does not change capability. Revoked paperwork does not disable a live credential. A court order does not rebuild enforcement. A real return of keys can succeed, and humans can refuse its responsibility. A verified breach calls for response without certifying domestic acceleration. Local repairs remain valuable after wider control is lost. Late alarms, regional rescue and nuclear catastrophe retain meaningful remaining choices; extinction ends living decisions.

Stage7 distinguishes recovery from erasure: maintainable benefits, relief versus harvest, tired human crews, revocable licences, comfortable powerlessness, protected remnants, oppressive human containment, records, appeals, distribution, maintained restraint, bounded research, irreversible deaths, institutional obligations and uncertain digital moral status. The final available act can stop one stipulated terminal release without pretending to restore government. The closing anchor resolves the actual surviving state.

## Review limits and release gates

Node review status is a content-review claim, not a claim that the entire causal model, art, accessibility, duration or deployment has been certified. All154 nodes cleared the documented editorial review and the coordinator authorized promotion to the story profile. Full engine and release gates remain separately recorded. Final release evidence belongs in `reports/validation/`, not in this document's node count.

The institution model is deliberately coarse. It has scoped grants and effective controllers, not a complete legal system, corporate succession graph or recursive agent hierarchy. In particular S5-02 records an onward-delegation policy and consequences; it does not implement separate per-agent appointment permissions. Several institutional choices affect broad facts used by later branches. These abstractions must remain explicit in receipts and analysis, and are not passed full-model distinctions under CR003.

Publication must still distinguish the complete content bank from full visual acceptance and from research validity. Anonymous run contributions are observational, self-selected and repeatable by one person; they cannot establish population moral preferences or empirical AI catastrophe probabilities.
