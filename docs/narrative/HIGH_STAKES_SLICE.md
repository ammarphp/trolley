# The price of the next signature

23 September 2026. Copy revision for the 14-node development slice. The PSA has a position: do not hand away effective control merely because the benefits are real, the quarterly numbers demand it, or someone says the rival will win. This is persuasive fiction. It is not a neutral opinion survey, an estimate of future catastrophe, or a claim that catastrophe is inevitable. The reader should feel the pressure before being asked to judge the decision.

The working clinic matters. The patients are not a trick. Later, preserving control costs jobs and delays homes; those people matter too. The comedy targets the language that makes those pressures convenient excuses: a safety certificate for the previous system, a zero-length approval queue, a mandatory human signature under a decision already made. The dead are not the punchline.

## Evidence, inference and fiction

**Recurrent depth and observability.** The [Geiping et al. prototype](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.05171v1) supports additional internal computation and reported benchmark gains. The [Anthropic author report](https://www.anthropic.com/research/reasoning-models-dont-say-think) supports a narrower warning about explanations omitting influences in controlled tasks. Combining these motivates the scene; neither establishes that recurrence causes unfaithfulness or that increased depth inevitably defeats oversight. Morrow's architecture, audit failure, rate of improvement and permissions are fictional. The mechanics still represent authority and capability separately.

**US–China pressure.** The [15 May 2024 NSC statement](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/statement-nsc-spokesperson-adrienne-watson-the-us-prc-talks-ai-risk-and-safety-0) documents safety dialogue amid competition. It does not reveal either country's actual capability lead or compliance. The scene invents both warnings and a future inspection offer; it does not quote either government. The available inspection choice stipulates real access at covered labs, not omniscience over every actor. Rejecting it cannot be portrayed as empirical proof that the rival was secretly ahead.

**The IPO and collateral harm.** The [November 2025 financial-stability survey](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/november-2025-financial-stability-report-near-term-risks-to-the-financial-system.htm) records market contacts' conditional concerns about AI investment sentiment and financial stress. It is not a Federal Reserve prediction. Vela's financing contract, the cancelled IPO, layoffs and interrupted housing are invented. Their cost uses the existing GDP decrement; there is no separate quantitative employment or housing simulation. This revision does not claim that one real-world cancelled offering would cause a national crisis.
